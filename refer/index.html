Eligibility

Check which referral route applies to your business

Existing Client Referral Programme

  • The Referrer must be an active Tacassure client in good standing.
  • The Referee must be a new prospect that is not already in active discussion with Tacassure.
  • The referral must be submitted before the prospective client contacts Tacassure directly.
  • The Referred company must have a genuine requirement for remote professional support.
  • The referral must include accurate business and contact information and have the Referee’s permission to be introduced.

Open Referral and Trial Programme

  • The Referrer is a genuine business and is not an existing Tacassure client when the referral is submitted.
  • The Referrer and the Referee are separate organisations and are both new to Tacassure.
  • The Referee has consented to the introduction and is not already recorded as an active opportunity.
  • The Referrer’s trial requirement is genuine, current, and supported by an authorised decision-maker.
  • The trial can be limited to one clearly defined task or deliverable and Tacassure has an appropriately skilled remote professional available.
  • The Referrer has realistic potential to proceed with an ongoing paid placement.

All referrals are reviewed by Tacassure. Submission does not guarantee a reward or trial.

Programme Terms

Rewards and trials qualify only after the Referee’s business signs an agreement and begins a paid placement.

Existing Client Referral Programme

  • The first five referrals earn 20 hours each, while the sixth through tenth provide 25 hours per referral. Any referral from the eleventh onward yields 30 hours.
  • No cap on successful referrals. Higher reward tiers may be approved.
  • Approved credit is deducted from the following week’s invoice. No separate redemption deadline applies.
  • Hours cannot be transferred, refunded, exchanged for cash, or applied retrospectively.
  • Self-referrals, duplicates, and existing pipeline referrals do not qualify.
  • Tacassure may verify, accept or decline referrals, and amend or withdraw the programme with reasonable notice.

Open Referral and Trial Programme

  • One trial per eligible Referrer’s business: up to 20 hours in one agreed working week.
  • The trial should be redeemed within three months of eligibility confirmation.
  • Scope, deliverables, schedule, access, and contact must be agreed in writing.
  • Subject to approval and professional availability. Additional work requires a separate quotation or paid agreement.
  • No carry-forward, transfers, refunds, or cash exchange.
  • Self-referrals, duplicates, and common-ownership referrals are excluded unless agreed otherwise in writing.
  • Tacassure may pause or end the trial if required access, information, or participation is not provided.
