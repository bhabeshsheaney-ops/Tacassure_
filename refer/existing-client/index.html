Existing Client Referral Program

Referral form

By filling out this form, you, the Referrer, confirm that you are currently a Tacassure client. Once the separate referred business signs an agreement and begins a paid placement, your company may receive 20 complimentary support hours for an existing outsourced remote professional with us.

Rewards Structure

1st – 5th Referral 20 hours each
6th – 10th Referral 25 hours each
11th – 15th Referral 30 hours each

The hours may provide additional project support, help manage increased workload, or extend the working time of your current Tacassure remote professional without an additional service charge.

Eligibility Criteria

  • The Referrer must be an active Tacassure client in good standing.
  • The Referee’s business must be a new prospect that is not already in active discussions with Tacassure.
  • The referral must be submitted before the Referee’s business contacts Tacassure directly.
  • The Referee’s business must have a genuine requirement for remote professional support.
  • The Referrer must provide accurate business and contact details, and obtain the referred contact’s permission for the introduction.

Terms and Conditions

  • Rewards are issued only after the referred business signs an agreement and begins a paid placement.
  • Each successful referral earns complimentary support hours according to the Referrer’s cumulative number of successful referrals: 20 hours for referrals 1–5, 25 hours for referrals 6–10, 30 hours for referrals 11–15. There is no cap on successful referrals, and higher reward tiers may be approved by Tacassure.
  • The value of the approved complimentary hours is deducted from the referring client’s invoice for the following week.
  • Complimentary hours cannot be transferred, refunded, exchanged for cash, or applied retrospectively.
  • No expiry date or redemption deadline applies, as the approved credit is applied automatically to the following week’s invoice.
  • Self-referrals, duplicate submissions, and referrals already recorded in Tacassure’s sales pipeline do not qualify.
  • Tacassure may verify, accept or decline referrals, and may amend or withdraw the programme with reasonable notice.
