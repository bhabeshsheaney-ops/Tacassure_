Open Referral and Trial Programme

Referral form

By filling out this form, you, the Referrer, confirm that you are not a Tacassure client and that you are separate from the referred business. Once that business signs an agreement and begins a paid placement, your business may qualify for a one-week trial of up to 20 hours, subject to eligibility, agreed scope, availability, and approval.

Requirement

Eligibility Criteria

  • The Referrer must be a genuine business and must not be an existing Tacassure client when the referral is submitted.
  • The Referrer and the Referee’s business must be separate organisations, both new to Tacassure.
  • The Referee must have agreed to the introduction, and the Referee’s business must not already be recorded as an active opportunity in Tacassure’s sales pipeline.
  • The Referrer must have a genuine, current requirement for trial remote professional support, supported by an authorised decision-maker.
  • The trial must cover one clearly defined task or deliverable.
  • Tacassure must have a suitably skilled professional available to deliver the trial.
  • The Referrer’s business must have realistic potential to proceed with an ongoing paid placement following the trial.

Terms and Conditions

  • An eligible Referrer’s business may obtain a complimentary trial once the separate Referee’s business has signed an agreement and started a paid placement. This trial applies to the Referrer’s business rather than the Referee’s.
  • The trial is limited to a maximum of 20 hours in total and does not constitute a recurring weekly entitlement.
  • The trial must be redeemed within three (3) months of Tacassure confirming eligibility.
  • All trial hours must be used within one (1) agreed working week and cannot be carried forward.
  • The scope, deliverables, schedule, access requirements, and designated client contact must be agreed in writing before the trial begins.
  • Work outside the agreed scope requires a separate quotation or paid agreement.
  • The trial is subject to professional availability and Tacassure’s approval.
  • Each business may receive only one (1) complimentary trial.
  • Trial hours cannot be transferred, refunded, or exchanged for cash.
  • Self-referrals, referrals between organisations under common ownership, and duplicate referrals do not qualify unless Tacassure agrees otherwise in writing.
  • Tacassure may pause or end the trial if the required access, information, or client participation is not provided.
